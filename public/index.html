Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about government passport photo standards.

Are photos generated here accepted by governments?
Yes! Cubit.pics formats photos according to official government dimensions (e.g., Bangladesh 45×55mm, US 2×2", Schengen 35×45mm), requiring neutral backgrounds and 60-70% face height ratios.
Is my uploaded photo sent to a server?
No! All background segmentation, face detection, and passport canvas rendering happen 100% locally in your web browser using WebGPU / WebAssembly. Your photo never leaves your device.
Can I print these photos on home printers or at photo shops?
Absolutely. Use the "Download Print Sheet" or "A4 Print Sheet Editor" to generate a 300 DPI print sheet tiled with 4, 8, 12, or 20 photo copies on standard A4 or 4×6 photo paper.
What expression and posture should I use in my photo?
Look directly into the camera with a neutral facial expression (mouth closed), eyes open and clearly visible. Avoid shadows on your face or background.

About

About Cubit.pics

Cubit.pics is a fully in-browser passport and visa photo studio. Our AI segmentation model runs locally on your device using WebGPU (with a WebAssembly fallback), so your photo never leaves your computer or phone. There are no uploads, no cloud processing, no accounts, and no tracking of your images — just an instant, professional result.

Before vs. After: Instant on-device background removal & AI formal dress try-on

Behind the scenes we combine a modern neural background-removal pipeline with an edge-aware compositor and a color-managed rendering engine. That means clean hair edges, accurate skin tones, and print-ready output at the exact millimeter and DPI specifications used by governments around the world — US, Schengen, UK, Canada, India, China, Australia, Japan and many more presets are built in.

You stay in full creative control. Adjust framing with live compliance guides, tune brightness, contrast, exposure, shadows, highlights, clarity and noise, pick a compliant white or off-white background (or any custom color), and export as JPEG or PNG. Need a physical copy? Generate a perfectly tiled 4×6″ print sheet ready for home printers or any photo kiosk — all in seconds, all on your device.
