Contact Passport Room

Email support@passport-room.com and a member of the team will reply, usually within two working days.

What to include

For a bug report, tell us your browser and operating system, the country preset you were using, and what happened versus what you expected. Please do not attach the photo itself — we do not need it and we would rather not have it.

Common reasons people write

  • A passport or visa size we do not have a preset for yet.
  • A cut-out, crop or enhance result that looks wrong.
  • Printing at the wrong physical size from the print sheet.
  • Privacy or data questions — see the privacy policy.
  • Press, partnership or advertising enquiries.

Send a message

This form opens your own email app with the details filled in; nothing is submitted to a Passport Room server.

Where we are

Passport Room is run by an independent team in Dhaka, Bangladesh. We work by email only, so support@passport-room.com is the fastest route to us.
