How points are calculated

Each partner reports a payout in money. That payout is multiplied by the rate shown on the partner card, and the result is capped per activity and per day by the limits in our configuration. The rate is applied by the server, never by the browser.

Getting your points

Points are not cash and cannot be transferred between accounts. When you have at least the minimum shown above, contact us from the same browser you earned them in and we will arrange the payout manually.

Fraud, reversals and limits

Partners can reverse a completion after the fact — for example when a survey is rejected for straight-lining or a payment is charged back. A reversal takes back the same points and appears in your history with its reason. Accounts that automate walls, use VPNs to fake a country or share a device to farm surveys are removed.

Rewards are operated by Passport Room with third-party partners. Read the terms of service and privacy policy. Nothing on this page is a promise of earnings: what you receive depends on which activities you complete and whether the partner confirms them.
