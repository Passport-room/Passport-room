Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how Passport Room handles photos, accounts, visitor information, advertising and payments.

1. Your photos are processed on your device

Every image operation in Passport Room — background removal, face detection, face enhance, cropping to a passport or visa size, colour and exposure adjustments, the outfit overlay, print-sheet layout and export — runs inside your web browser on the device you are using. The app has no photo upload endpoint: photos you open are not sent to or stored on our servers. If you choose to save photo history, a thumbnail remains in this browser until you delete it or clear site data. Downloaded files remain wherever you save them.

2. Network requests the app does make

The AI features need model files, and those are downloaded to your browser the first time you use each feature, then cached on your device for later visits. Your photo is not part of these requests; they are ordinary file downloads. The files are fetched from:

  • huggingface.co — the MODNet background-segmentation model, the YOLOFace face-detection model and the GPEN-BFR-256 face-restoration model.
  • cdn.jsdelivr.net — the onnxruntime-web runtime and WebAssembly binaries that execute those models, and the three.js library used by the decorative animation.
  • fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com — the site's web fonts.
  • Google Firebase — account sign-in, visitor records, reviews and Pro access.
  • ipwho.is — a country lookup using your IP address for visitor statistics.
  • Google AdSense — delivery and measurement of advertising for free visitors.

As with any web request, those third parties can see your IP address and browser user agent when a file is fetched. They receive no image data. Their own privacy terms apply to those requests.

3. Data stored in your browser

The app keeps local profile details (including any avatar you set), editor preferences, usage counters, a visitor identifier and number, and your sign-in session in browser storage. If you enable photo history, recent photo thumbnails are also saved locally. Clearing browser site data removes this local information, including your local photo history, but does not delete a signed-in account, visitor records or purchase records held by service providers. You can turn off or clear saved photo history in the app.

4. Accounts, visitor statistics and reviews

You can use the free photo tools without signing in. If you choose to sign up or sign in, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email address, password credentials or Google sign-in details, account identifier, display name, profile image and verification status as applicable. We keep a sign-in session on your device. Google handles authentication data under its own privacy policy; we do not store your password in the site's visitor records.

We assign each browser a persistent random device identifier and customer number, and store a visitor record in Firebase Realtime Database. It includes device type, browser, operating system, screen size, user agent, country, visit count, time spent, photo-export count and visit dates. A request to ipwho.is uses your IP address to look up your country; the country is cached locally. This is not fully anonymous: a persistent identifier can distinguish return visits. If you submit a community review, we store its rating, text, timestamp, device identifier and customer number and display it publicly under a customer label.

5. Advertising

Free visitors may see Google AdSense ads in the studio, including around the editing and download experience. Pro members do not see these ad placements. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers and information about visits to deliver, measure and personalise ads, subject to applicable consent requirements. You can manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center and read the Google privacy policy. Ad requests do not include the photo opened in the editor.

6. Payments and Passport Room Pro

Passport Room Pro is an optional one-off purchase of US$0.99 for 30 days of unlimited, ad-free exports. Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, which is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and handles payment, tax, invoicing and billing support. Your card details are entered into Paddle's own secure checkout and are never seen, received or stored by Passport Room.

When a payment succeeds, Paddle sends us a notification containing your purchase email address, a transaction identifier and the purchase date. We retain purchase and licence information such as the email used at checkout, transaction reference, purchase and expiry dates, and access status in Firebase Realtime Database so the app can confirm or restore Pro access. We do not receive or store card details. You can request deletion of information we control by emailing support.passport.room@gmail.com. Paddle's own handling of your data is described in the Paddle privacy policy. Refund terms are set out in our refund policy.

7. Children

Passport Room is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. Children should not create an account or submit a review without a parent or guardian. Account, visitor and purchase information may be collected as described above; we do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian is welcome to use the tool to produce a child's passport photo, and that photo is handled exactly as described in section 1.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may ask for access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of any data we hold about you. This may include account information, visitor records, reviews and purchase records. You can remove local data by clearing site data in your browser; that does not erase remote records. For an account or data request, including deletion, write to support.passport.room@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. Billing records held by Paddle as Merchant of Record are requested from Paddle directly.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Photos opened in the editor are processed locally, but account, visitor, review and purchase information is processed by the providers listed below. Keep your browser and device updated; signed-in sessions and saved photo history may remain on shared computers.

10. Legal bases and how long we keep data

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal basis depends on the activity. Account access and Pro purchases are necessary to provide the services you request; security and basic site statistics may rely on legitimate interests; tax and accounting records may be required by law. Advertising cookies and personalisation may require your consent under applicable law. See section 5 for Google's advertising controls.

  • Photos and edits: not uploaded to us; optional saved thumbnails remain on your device until you delete them or clear site data.
  • Account, visitor and review information: retained while needed to provide accounts, measure site use and display reviews, subject to deletion requests and applicable legal obligations.
  • Pro licence record (order reference, licence key, start and end date, email supplied at checkout): kept while the pass is active and for up to 5 years afterwards for tax, accounting and dispute handling, as required by law.
  • Payment and invoicing records: held by Paddle as Merchant of Record under its own policy and retention rules.
  • Server request logs (IP address, timestamp, requested path) at our hosting provider: kept for a short period for security and abuse prevention, then deleted.

11. International transfers and the companies that process data for us

Our hosting, authentication, visitor analytics, payment and advertising providers operate outside Bangladesh, so data such as your IP address, device identifiers and account or checkout email may be processed abroad, including in the United States and the European Union, under the safeguards those providers publish — typically Standard Contractual Clauses. The processors we use are:

  • Paddle.com Market Ltd — Merchant of Record: payment, fraud checks, tax and invoicing.
  • Our hosting and CDN provider — serving the site and delivering the AI model files.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd) — Authentication for optional accounts and the Realtime Database holding visitor statistics, reviews and Pro licence records described in sections 4 and 6.
  • Google Fonts — serving the web fonts used on the site.
  • Google AdSense — advertising in the studio for free visitors, as described in section 5.
  • ipwho.is — country lookup from your IP address for visitor statistics.
  • Hugging Face and jsDelivr — one-time downloads of the AI model files and libraries; these requests never contain image data.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for any purpose not listed here.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The date above shows when this text was last revised.

13. Who we are and how to contact us

The data controller is Passport Room (legal name: Passport Room), Jamalpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh. Email support.passport.room@gmail.com for any privacy question, access, correction, deletion or objection request; we reply within 2 business days and complete requests within 30 days. Full business and billing details are on our contact page. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to your local data protection authority.
