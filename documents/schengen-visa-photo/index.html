Photos that pass vs. photos that get rejected

Pass: sharp focus, eyes open and level, neutral expression, even light, plain background, taken within 6 months.
Rejected: shadows on face or background, tilted head, glasses glare, filters or beauty edits, hair covering eyes, wrong head size.
