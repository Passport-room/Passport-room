Photos that pass vs. photos that get rejected

Pass: sharp focus, eyes open and level, neutral expression, even light, plain background, taken within 6 months.
Rejected: shadows on face or background, tilted head, glasses glare, filters or beauty edits, hair covering eyes, wrong head size.

Frequently asked questions

What is the DV lottery photo size?

Exactly 600×600 pixels, JPEG format, maximum 240 KB, with the head filling 50–69 % of the height.

Can I reuse an old photo for the DV lottery?

No. The photo must be taken within the last 6 months; reused photos can disqualify the entry.

Always confirm the latest rules with the official issuing authority before submitting — requirements can change.
