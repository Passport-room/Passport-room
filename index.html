About Make.pics

Make.pics creates compliant passport and visa photos in seconds. AI background removal runs entirely on-device in your browser, so your photo is processed locally and never uploaded to a server. Adjust framing, zoom, and rotation, then export in the size and format you need.

  • On-device AI background removal
    Private by design — your image never leaves this device.
  • Adjustable framing, zoom & rotation
    Dial in the perfect crop with live guides.
  • Multiple passport & visa presets
    US, Schengen, UK, India, Canada, China, Australia and more.
  • Download as JPEG or PNG
    High-resolution files ready for online applications.
  • 4×6″ print sheet option
    Auto-tiled layout for one-click home or kiosk printing.
