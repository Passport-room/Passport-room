United Kingdom passport photo requirements

A summary of the official United Kingdom passport photo rules, with a link to the government source. Last reviewed: 4 November 2026.

Official requirements (verified)

Source: GOV.UK — Get a passport photo. Rules change; the official page always takes priority over this summary.

  • Digital photos must be taken in the last month.
  • Clear, in focus, in colour and unaltered by computer software.
  • At least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels tall, and between 50 KB and 10 MB.
  • Plain light-coloured background in clear contrast to you, with no other objects or people and no red eye.
  • Face forwards with a plain expression, mouth closed, eyes open and visible, no hair over the eyes and no shadows on your face or behind you.
  • No head covering unless for religious or medical reasons. Do not wear glasses unless you have to; never sunglasses or tinted glasses.
  • For photos taken on your own device during the application, include head, shoulders and upper body and do not crop it yourself.

Important: edited photos and background removal
