Canada passport photo requirements

A summary of the official Canada passport photo rules, with a link to the government source. Last reviewed: 4 November 2026.

Official requirements (verified)

Source: Government of Canada — Passport photos. Rules change; the official page always takes priority over this summary.

  • Size: 50 mm wide by 70 mm high (2 × 2¾ inches).
  • Face height: 31 mm (1¼ in) to 36 mm (1⁷⁄₁₆ in) from chin to crown.
  • Taken no more than 6 months before you submit your application.
  • Colour or black and white.
  • Professionally printed on plain, high-quality photographic paper.
  • Plain white or light-coloured background.
  • No sunglasses or tinted glasses.

Important: edited photos and background removal

The Government of Canada gives cutting and pasting your image against a white background as an example of an unacceptable change.
