United States passport photo requirements

A summary of the official United States passport photo rules, with a link to the government source. Last reviewed: 4 November 2026.

Official requirements (verified)

Source: U.S. Department of State — Passport photos. Rules change; the official page always takes priority over this summary.

  • Printed size: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm).
  • Head size: 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm) from the bottom of the chin to the top of the head.
  • One colour photo taken in the last 6 months.
  • Plain white or off-white background, free of shadows, textures or objects.
  • Submit the original, unedited photo without filters or digital changes.
  • Eyeglasses must be removed.
  • No photocopies or digitally scanned photos.
  • Online renewals use an uploaded digital photo; see the State Department’s digital photo page.

Important: edited photos and background removal

The U.S. Department of State says not to change a passport photo with software, apps or filters, and shows a photo with a digitally cropped background as an example of what is not acceptable.
